The atelier

We buy less, and we buy better.

Mira started as a weekend market stall in Portland. We still work with the same four workshops — a chair maker in Oregon, a potter in Oaxaca, a weaver in Kyoto, and a brass studio in Jaipur.

Every piece is numbered, repairable, and meant to outlast the season it arrived in.

“A chair should feel inevitable. You sit down and forget you are sitting.”

— Elena Voss, founder
